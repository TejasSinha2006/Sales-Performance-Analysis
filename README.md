# Sales-Performance-Analysis
Sales Performance Analysis for Alfido Tech Data Analyst Internship. Includes sales analysis, profit analysis, regional analysis, discount analysis, customer segment analysis, and business recommendations.
# Sales Performance Analysis

## Objective
Analyze sales data to identify high-performing categories, profitable regions, customer segments, and business growth opportunities.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed
- Data Cleaning
- Sales Analysis
- Profit Analysis
- Regional Analysis
- Discount Analysis
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Monthly Sales Trends

## Key Findings
- Office Supplies generated the highest sales and profit.
- Consumer segment contributed the highest revenue.
- Higher discounts significantly reduced profitability.
- Central region achieved the highest sales.
- Sales peaked during the final months of the year.

## Recommendations
1. Focus on Office Supplies.
2. Improve Furniture profitability.
3. Reduce excessive discounting.
4. Expand in weak regions.
5. Strengthen Consumer customer retention.
